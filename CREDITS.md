## Major foundations

Eldritch Aeons depends on the work of many Minecraft mod authors. Major gameplay foundations include:

- Thaumaturge and KubeThaum
- Build a Spell
- Occultism and Occultism KubeJS
- Ice and Fire Community Edition
- The Twilight Forest
- Vampirism
- FTB Quests, Teams, Chunks, and Essentials
- Tom's Simple Storage
- Apothic Attributes, Enchanting, and Spawners
- When Dungeons Arise and Seven Seas
- JEI, KubeJS, LootJS, Patchouli, Modonomicon, and their libraries

This list is not exhaustive. The CurseForge manifest and in-game Mods screen provide the authoritative list of included projects. All third-party projects retain their own authorship and licenses.
