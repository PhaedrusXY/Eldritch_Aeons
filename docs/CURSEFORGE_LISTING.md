## Long description

# Eldritch Aeons

**Study the world. Name its laws. Break them carefully.**

Eldritch Aeons is a dark-fantasy magic and exploration modpack for Minecraft 26.1.2 on NeoForge. Its central goal is to make multiple magic and adventure mods feel like parts of the same occult world rather than unrelated systems placed beside one another.

You begin with limited tools and incomplete knowledge. Progress comes from scanning the world, performing research, inscribing runes, binding spirits, exploring dangerous structures, and mastering increasingly unstable forms of magic.

## Signature features

### A researched spell language

Build a Spell has been reworked into **Arcane Syntax**. Instead of accumulating essence and receiving random spell components, you craft exact Inscribed Runes and permanently learn their concepts.

- 75 crafted delivery, effect, and modifier runes
- Four progression tiers: Foundation, Structured, Infused, and Forbidden
- Arcane Workbench inscriptions, Occultism ritual inscriptions, and Thaumaturge infusion inscriptions
- Custom wands, research, textures, tooltips, and balance rules
- Capstone syntax including Teleport, Summon, Echo, Split, Double, and Fill

### Dragonsteel as magical engineering

**Draconic Artifice** turns Dragonsteel into a late-game achievement. Dragonforge components require Dreadstone, Thaumium, dragon materials, and the Thaumaturge Infusion Matrix. The forge transforms Thaumium—not ordinary iron—under sustained dragon breath.

### Thaumaturgy integrated with storage

The included Thaumic Storage Scanning compatibility addon allows Thaumometer research directly inside Tom's Simple Storage. Previously scanned items display their aspects, while unknown items can be scanned without manually moving stacks into the player inventory.

### A connected dark-fantasy world

Explore the Twilight Forest, raid enormous structures, confront dragons, engage with Vampirism, develop Apothic equipment, bind Occultism spirits, and follow custom quests and research that connect the pack's systems.

## Designed for

- Players who enjoy magic progression, research, and discovery
- Solo worlds and small multiplayer servers
- Dangerous exploration without abandoning quality-of-life storage and navigation
- Players who prefer deliberate cross-mod progression over unrestricted kitchen-sink crafting
