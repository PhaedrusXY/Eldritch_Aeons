# Runic Mastery — syntactic phrases

This is the v1.2 Thaumonomicon text added with Arcane Syntax research 1.0.7.

> At first I thought these runes of power behaved like numbers in an equation: discrete terms that could be strung together in any order, each simply adding its effect to the result. I no longer believe that. They are closer to words. Certain combinations, arranged as a complete expression, resolve into a meaning greater and more specific than their parts. The clearest example I have found is Launch → Levitation → Slow Fall. Separately they describe propulsion, suspension, and a gentle descent. Spoken together, the grammar resolves into a single idea: Creative Flight. The spell does not merely perform three effects. It understands the sentence.

The example reflects Build a Spell's built-in Creative Flight combo requirements: Launch, Levitation, and Slow Fall. The prose presents the components as a phrase for narrative clarity without asserting that literal component-slot order is universally required by the combo matcher.
